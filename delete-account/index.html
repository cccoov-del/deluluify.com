Delete your Deluluify account

You can delete your Deluluify account and everything in it at any time. It takes a few seconds.

In the app

  1. Open Deluluify.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account & data, then Yes, delete my account.

Can’t open the app?

Email support@deluluify.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll delete it within 30 days and let you know when it’s done.

What gets deleted

Your profile, dreams, Solulus, projects and numbers, steps, check-ins, Bestie chats, your selfie, and the images and videos stored on our servers. Boards you posted to Manifest are removed too. Backups roll off within 30 days.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, as the law requires. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription, so cancel it in Google Play or the App Store too.
