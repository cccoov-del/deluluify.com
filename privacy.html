Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Deluluify (“we”, “us”) helps you turn a big dream into daily steps. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. Questions: support@deluluify.com.

What we collect

  • Account: a random account ID created when you start. If you choose to save your account, your email address and a password (stored securely by our database provider; we never see it in plain text).
  • What you tell the app: your name or nickname, your dream (“delulu”) in your words, your numbers for the delulu math, “your life right now”, your interests, answers to Bestie’s questions, plans, daily steps (“solulus”), check-ins and notes.
  • Bestie chats: messages you send to Bestie and her replies (only if you turn on the AI Bestie).
  • Your projects and money numbers: the projects you track or tell Bestie about (names, what they are, prices, goals), and the numbers you or Bestie log (revenue, paying customers, sales, installs), plus Bestie’s projections and ideas.
  • Your Solulus: what you tell Bestie before she researches your dream (where you’re at, your answers, the plan you agree on), the researched plans, deep dives and weekly updates she writes, and which steps you tick off.
  • AI images: the scene descriptions you ask us to turn into images, and the resulting images.
  • Purchases: your subscription status and sparkle balance. Payments are handled by Apple or Google and our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never see your card details.

What stays on your phone: voice recordings of your affirmations are stored only on your device and are not uploaded. A selfie you add also stays on your device, unless you turn on “You in your dream life”.

Manifest (community): boards you post and boards you manifest are public. When you manifest a board, a mini profile shows to others: your first name, an emoji avatar, an optional one-line intro, your manifesting streak, the boards you manifest and the boards you posted. Tapping “I manifested today” shows others that you manifested that day. There are no messages between users. Stop manifesting a board to leave it, and deleting your account removes all of it.

“You in your dream life” (Premium, optional): if you turn it on and agree, your selfie is stored privately in our storage and sent to our image AI provider (fal.ai) only when you make one of your own dream images. Images with your face are private to you and can never be posted to the community board. Turning the feature off, deleting your selfie or deleting your account removes it from our servers.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: save your progress, size your daily steps, unlock scenes and sync across devices.
  • To provide AI features you choose to use: Bestie, Solulus and their research, projections and ideas, dream images and videos.
  • To send account emails (like password resets) and other important messages.
  • To keep the service safe (for example, daily usage limits) and to answer support requests.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our contract with you, your consent for optional AI processing, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Who helps us (processors)

  • Supabase — database, accounts and file storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — powers Bestie, Solulus, projections and ideas. We send only what’s needed: your dream, your answers, your plan, your projects and numbers, and your message. When Bestie researches your dream she uses Anthropic’s web search; searches are about your dream and field, never your name or contact details.
  • fal.ai — creates AI dream images and short dream videos from the scene description you write, and, only if you turn on “You in your dream life”, from your selfie.
  • Resend — sends account emails.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and the web app.
  • Apple, Google and RevenueCat — process and keep track of payments and subscriptions.

Some providers process data outside your country (including in the United States) under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), private storage with server-side access rules (only you can read your own data), and passwords that are never stored in plain text. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so if you think your account has been accessed by someone else, email us right away.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, dreams, Solulus, projects and numbers, steps, check-ins, chats and stored images and videos from our servers. Backups roll off within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete my account.
  • AI off: turn the AI Bestie off in Settings at any time.
  • Access, correction, export or objection: email us. If you’re in the EU/UK you can also complain to your data-protection authority.

Age

Deluluify is for people aged 16 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16, and we delete such accounts when we learn about them. If you think someone under 16 is using Deluluify, email us.

Health, money and other sensitive topics

If you write about your health, weight or money, we use it only to make your plan. Deluluify gives general information, not medical, financial, legal or immigration advice.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll post the new version here with a new date and tell you in the app.

Contact

Email support@deluluify.com, or use Settings in the app.
